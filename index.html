    How to play

    Pick a move. The machine picks at the same moment, and it never sees your current move, only the ones you've already played.

    • Stone crushes scissors.
    • Scissors cut paper.
    • Paper covers stone.
    • If you both pick the same move, it's a draw. Draws don't score, so throw again.

    Winning a match

    Best of 3 means the first to win 2 rounds takes the match. Best of 5 needs 3 wins and best of 7 needs 4. Endless keeps a running score until you start a new match.

    The two opponents

    Random picks every move by chance, so nothing you do changes your odds.

    Adaptive watches the order you play your moves in and tries to counter the one you're likely to play next. After each round it tells you what it predicted. Play unpredictably to beat it.

    Controls

    Tap a move. On a keyboard, press 1 for stone, 2 for paper and 3 for scissors.
